04 — A FEW THINGS BEFORE YOU GO

CURIOUS?
GOOD.

Even a Monkey King starts with questions.

What is Wukong?+

Wukong is a digital art and NFT concept inspired by Sun Wukong from Journey to the West. We are building a playful character universe through stylized 3D art, hand-drawn 2D illustrations, and imaginative storytelling.

Are these 2D or 3D characters?+

Both! The collection explores rendered 3D character designs and illustrated 2D versions. The current site displays concept artwork; the 3D-style artwork is not a live, rotatable 3D model.

Can I mint a Wukong now?+

Not yet. This is a concept preview. The blockchain, supply, price, and mint date have not been announced. No minting, payment, or wallet signing is enabled here.

How do the characters move?+

Explore the layered scene with your cursor or touch, scroll to move the camera closer, and click the hero scene for a magical burst. These are animated artwork layers, rather than a fully rigged 3D character. Use the FX button to pause effects.
